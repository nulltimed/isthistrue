Corrections policy

We will make mistakes. What matters is what happens next: here no correction is ever hidden, because every claim page keeps its full revision history, public and linked at the top of the page itself. A corrected verdict does not silently replace the previous one: both remain visible, with dates.

How to request a correction

  1. ▼ votes on the sentence: if an analysed sentence accumulates negative votes from the community, the system automatically relaunches the deep re-analysis of that sentence. This is the normal path and requires nothing else.
  2. The post's forum thread: every video has its thread; posting a source there that contradicts the verdict is the fastest way to get moderation to look at it.
  3. The complaints form (complaints): for errors that are not about verdicts (speaker attribution, personal data, unlawful content) or if you prefer a direct channel.

What we do with it

  • If the new evidence changes the verdict, the re-analysis is published as a new version in the page's history, with its sources.
  • If it does not, the page is left untouched — but the forum thread keeps the debate and anyone can judge for themselves.
  • Operational errors (transcription, voices, dates) are fixed and logged in the project's technical record, which is public in the repository.

This policy is part of our path towards the International Fact-Checking Network standards (about us).
