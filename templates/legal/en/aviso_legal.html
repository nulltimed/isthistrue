What this site is

esestocierto? analyses public videos with the help of AI and the community: it transcribes what is said, identifies verifiable claims and checks them against linked, reproducible sources following a documented methodology. Claims are verified, never people, and no result is a value judgement about anyone.

No third-party media is stored: videos are displayed through each platform's official player. No voiceprints or any other biometric data are stored.
