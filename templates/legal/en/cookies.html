Cookie policy

This site uses only strictly necessary technical cookies, the kind the law does not require consent for because the site cannot work without them:

  • sessionid — keeps you signed in.
  • csrftoken — protects forms against forgery.
  • language — remembers the interface language you chose.

No advertising or analytics cookies, ours or anyone else's: nobody tracks you on this site.

One external service may set its own technical cookies when it loads: Cloudflare Turnstile (registration anti-abuse, when active). Once the operating company's payment gateway is live, its provider may set cookies during checkout and will be detailed here. See their policies; we do not read those cookies.

Since there are only technical cookies, no consent banner is shown: there is nothing to consent to or refuse.
