Privacy policy

Data controller

David Souto Apariz (the non-profit project esestocierto?; an association is being constituted). Contact for anything related to your data: webmaster@esestocierto.com.

What we process and why

  • Account: email, username, password (always encrypted) and date of birth — only to verify the minimum age of 14 (art. 7 LOPDGDD) and access to adult content.
  • Activity: your posts, votes, messages and settings, to provide the service, moderate and keep the platform secure.
  • Optional profile: avatar and signature, if you set them.

Legal basis: performance of the service you request when registering, and legitimate interest in security. No advertising profiles, no selling of data, no third-party analytics.

What we do NOT process

Video analyses process public content (what is said in a published video), not our users' personal data: AI providers are never told who you are or what you do on the platform. No voiceprints or biometric data are stored.

Processors

  • IONOS — server hosting (European Union).
  • Brevo — transactional email (verification, notices).
  • Anthropic — AI analysis of the public content of videos (USA).
  • AssemblyAI — transcription and speaker separation of public videos' audio (USA). Audio is not kept after processing.
  • Runpod — on-demand GPU audio processing; containers are destroyed after each job and nothing persists.
  • PayPal — donations; PayPal processes your payment data under its own policy (we never see or store card data).
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — registration anti-abuse, when active.

Transfers outside the EU rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or each provider's current adequacy framework).

For how long

While your account exists. If you delete it, the account disappears and your messages become anonymous. Technical security logs are kept only as long as strictly needed.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability. The essentials need no email: delete (My account → Delete my account), portability (My account → Export my data, JSON download), rectify (edit your data in My account). For anything else: webmaster@esestocierto.com. Supervisory authority: the Spanish AEPD (aepd.es).
