About us

esestocierto? is a community fact-checking platform assisted by artificial intelligence: anyone brings a video, the system extracts its claims one by one, checks them against real sources and publishes each verdict — green, amber, red or grey — with its sources linked and a full public revision history. The community decides with its votes what gets deep verification, and every sentence can be discussed in the forum.

How we work

  • Translator and forensic analyst, never a judge of opinions: factual claims are checked against sources; opinions are analysed only for their logic and are always labelled as opinions.
  • Everything leaves a trail: every verdict lists its sources, every revision stays in the public history of its page, and the project's spending is published as monthly totals by category in the spending ledger.
  • The method, in writing: it is fully described in the methodology, and corrections are requested as explained in the corrections policy.
  • Open source (AGPL): anyone can audit how each verdict is reached.

Towards the IFCN standards

We are working to meet the International Fact-Checking Network code of principles: non-partisanship, transparency of sources, funding and methodology, and an open and honest corrections policy. This page, together with the methodology, the transparency page and the corrections policy, is part of that path.
