What can I post?

Paste the link of a video, a podcast or an audio file and the system will transcribe it, separate the voices and extract its claims. These are the platforms with an embedded player; any other video or audio URL is still analyzed and shown as a link card.

Transcription, voice separation and signals run automatically on posting. Source-based verification opens with community votes and identified speakers. Paid, private or live streams cannot be analyzed.
