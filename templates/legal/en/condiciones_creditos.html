Credit purchase terms

What a credit is

One credit equals €1 and is bought in prepaid packs. Credits buy immediacy, never outcomes: fast-tracking a queued analysis, launching source-checking without waiting for community votes, requesting a claim's origin chain, or relaunching a sentence with the premium model. Regular analysis is and will remain free: votes request it and the project budget pays for it.

What a credit does not buy

No payment alters a verdict. A paid re-analysis may confirm the existing verdict: you pay for the review, not the result. The quality gate (speaker identification) cannot be bought either.

Right of withdrawal

As immediately-executed digital content, by purchasing you expressly consent to credits being available instantly and accept that once a credit is SPENT you lose the right of withdrawal over it (art. 103.m of the Spanish Consumer Protection Act). Unspent credits are refundable: you may withdraw within 14 calendar days and recover the amount of any credits you have not used, by writing to the contact address.

Automatic refunds

Beyond withdrawal, the system refunds credits on its own whenever what you paid for is not delivered: if a fast-tracked analysis ends up failing, or an origin trace documents nothing, the credits return to your wallet with an entry in your history.

Prices and quotas

Current packs and prices are always shown on the credits page before buying, and every button that spends credits shows the cost and your balance and asks for confirmation. Prices may change; purchased credits do not expire.
